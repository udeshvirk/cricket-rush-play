Privacy

Beta build · updated 22 November 2026

Stumpflare is a cricket game that runs in your browser or in the Android beta app. This build has no account system, advertising SDK, in-app purchases or game analytics SDK.

Your game stays on your device

Settings, scores, records, saved matches and tournament progress are stored locally using browser storage. The game does not upload those records to a game server. Progress is not automatically shared between browsers or between the browser and Android app.

Your player’s photo

If you add a photo to one of your own players (My players), the game finds the face in it, crops and shrinks it, and builds the player’s 3D face from it, all on your device. The face finder’s files come from the game’s own site the first time you add a photo; the photo itself is not sent with them. The cropped photo and the face points measured from it are kept in the same local storage as your players. The photo is never uploaded, sent to a server or shared, and the game does not use the camera unless you choose to take a picture. It is only used to show your player in this game. Remove it with Remove photo or Remove player, or by clearing the game’s data as described below.

Optional spoken commentary

When you enable commentary, the browser uses its speech service to speak game-generated text, including fictional player names and match scores. Some voices work locally; others may send that text to the browser or operating-system provider’s speech service. That provider’s privacy terms apply. Commentary does not request microphone access or record your voice. You can turn it off in Settings. Voice availability differs across devices, and the Android beta may not support browser speech.

Web hosting

The browser version downloads game files from its host. The hosting provider may process standard connection data such as IP address, browser information and request logs. The public game is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with an existing GitHub Pages copy also available. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement. The Android beta bundles the game files and does not need to download them to play.

Deleting your progress

To remove all local game data in a browser, clear site data for the game’s address. On Android, use system Settings → Apps → Stumpflare → Storage → Clear storage. These actions delete progress. Uninstalling the Android beta also removes its local app data; uninstalling a browser-installed web app may require a separate site-data clear.

External links and future changes

Opening external links is subject to the destination’s policies. If a future release adds online accounts, analytics, ads or purchases, this notice will be updated before those features launch.

Contact

For support or general privacy questions, use the Stumpflare issue tracker. Issues are public: please do not include private personal information. GitHub’s privacy terms apply when you use that service.
