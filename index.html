Batting: choose Defend, Shot or Loft before the ball arrives. As it reaches the bat, swipe anywhere in the direction you want it to go: down the screen is straight back past the bowler, left is the off side, right is the leg side, up is behind you. Loft goes in the air: more runs, but it can be caught.

Keyboard (like Cricket 07): hold the arrow keys for the direction (two together for in-between, such as Down + Left for a cover drive) and press S as the ball arrives. S with no arrow defends. Hold Shift to loft it. D steps out of the crease (then S to hit; miss it and you can be stumped). A leaves the ball, or ducks a bouncer. Before the bowler runs in, Q and E move you across the crease.

Touch: the ◀ ▶ buttons move you across the crease before the bowler runs in; STEP OUT charges down the pitch. The circle on the pitch shows where the ball will bounce.

Bowling yourself: before each over, pick a bowler and a field. Tap the pitch where you want the ball to land, choose a variation, press BOWL, then press again when the moving needle is in the green.

QUICK CHASE

Choose Defend, Shot or Loft, then as the ball reaches the bat swipe anywhere where you want it to go: down the screen is straight, left is the off side, right is the leg side, up is behind you. A plain tap lets your timing choose. Loft goes in the air for more runs, but fielders can catch it. Watch the ball's shadow to judge the bounce.

Keyboard: hold arrows for the direction and press S to hit (S alone defends), Shift to loft, D to step out, A to leave, Q/E to move across before the run-up.
