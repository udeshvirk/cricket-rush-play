Short description
Cricket under the lights. Bat, bowl and chase glory.

Light up every innings in Stumpflare, an indie cricket game for phones and desktops. Time your shots, aim for gaps and mix up your bowling. Take on quick chases, play complete matches and mini series, or guide a fictional club through a tournament.

Features include touch and keyboard controls, 3D match presentation, automatic replays, optional commentary where browser speech is supported, and progress saved on your device. The browser version supports offline play after its game files have been cached.

This is an early-access beta. Animation, device performance and commentary are still being refined. No real league affiliation or official player likenesses are claimed.
